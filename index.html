About Onam

Discover the story behind Kerala's most celebrated festival.

The Festival of Joy

Onam is one of the most important festivals celebrated in Kerala. It brings families and communities together with colorful celebrations, traditional games, music, dance and delicious food.

The festival is traditionally associated with the return of King Mahabali, whose reign is remembered as a golden age of prosperity and happiness.
